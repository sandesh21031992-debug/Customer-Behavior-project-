# Customer-Behavior-project-
In this Power BI Project giving a comprehensive analysis of customer Behavior data, showing valuable insights on the customer behavior in the market, purchasing nature of the customer and what is the review on the different products , subscription and payment methods  

# Kye Insights
:Subscription wise customer :- In the first visual customers has been divided into two group subscribed and unsubscribed.
:Category wise purchasing behaviour :- This visual  shows that what is the product category wise purchasing behavior of the customer. 
:Purchase payment Method :- This visual is showing what is the payment method has been used by the customer.
:Gender Wise Purchases :- Visual is describing that how the gender wise purchase is gone through.
:Season wise Purchase :- in this visual has been described that what is the purchasing nature at the time of season changes.
:Age Group wise purchasing :- In this visual the customers are divided into group age wise.
